02 / BEHIND THE BUILDS

Curiosity first.
Build, learn, repeat.

I'm a Grade 9 student at AKM HSS Kottoor in Kerala. I learn best when I have something to build.

I enjoy connecting hardware and software: giving a robot a useful role, making a website easier to use or bringing a simulation to life. Robotics, AI and electronics give me plenty of questions to explore.

Through school projects and technology competitions, I'm learning to test ideas, work through problems and improve what I make. For HerbiQ, I worked with Team Delta Malabar on team coordination, AI integration and plant research. There's always a next version.
